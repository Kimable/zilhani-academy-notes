# **🌡️ 2.3 Transfer of Thermal Energy**

## **🔑 Key Idea**

Thermal energy (heat) is transferred from **hotter regions to colder regions** by three main processes:

* **Conduction**  
* **Convection**  
* **Radiation**

# **🔥 2.3.1 Conduction**

## **✅ Definition**

**Conduction** is the transfer of thermal energy through a substance **without bulk movement** of the substance.

## **🧪 Experiments: Good vs Bad Conductors**

### **🔬 Example Experiment 1: Metal Rod Test**

* Attach drawing pins to a metal rod using wax.  
* Heat one end of the rod.  
* Pins fall off in order.

**Observation:**

* Heat travels along the rod → pins drop sequentially.

**Conclusion:**

* Metals are **good thermal conductors**.

### **🔬 Example Experiment 2: Comparing Materials**

* Use rods of different materials (copper, glass, wood).  
* Heat one end.

**Observation:**

* Copper heats fastest → best conductor  
* Glass/wood heat slowly → insulators

## **⚛️ How Conduction Works (Particle Model)**

### **🧱 In ALL solids:**

* Particles vibrate more when heated.  
* Vibrations pass energy to neighboring particles.

### **⚡ In Metals (Important\!)**

* Metals have **free (delocalised) electrons**.  
* These electrons:  
  * Move quickly through the metal  
  * Carry thermal energy faster than vibrations

👉 This is why metals conduct heat **better than non-metals**.

## **🌬️ Why Conduction is Poor in Gases & Liquids**

* Particles are **far apart**  
* Fewer collisions → slower energy transfer

👉 Therefore:

* Gases \= very poor conductors  
* Most liquids \= poor conductors

## **📝 Key Fact**

* Some solids (e.g. glass) conduct heat better than gases but worse than metals.


## **📌 Exam Tips (Conduction)**

* Always mention:  
  * **Particle vibrations**  
  * **Free electrons (for metals)**  
* Don’t say particles “move along” → they **vibrate in place**

# **🌊 2.3.2 Convection**

## **✅ Definition**

**Convection** is the transfer of thermal energy by the **movement of fluids (liquids or gases)**.

## **🌍 Where It Happens**

* Only in **liquids and gases**  
* Not in solids

## **⚙️ How Convection Works**

### **Step-by-step:**

1. Fluid is heated → particles gain energy  
2. Fluid **expands** → density decreases  
3. Warm fluid rises  
4. Cooler fluid sinks  
5. Cycle repeats → **convection current**

## **🧪 Experiment: Convection in Water**

### **🔬 Setup:**

* Add potassium permanganate crystals to water  
* Heat gently from below

**Observation:**

* Colored currents show movement of water

👉 This visualizes convection currents.

## **🌬️ Example: Air Convection**

* Hot air rises (less dense)  
* Cool air sinks (more dense)

## **📝 Key Points**

* Convection depends on **density differences**  
* Causes **circular flow (currents)**

## **📌 Exam Tips (Convection)**

* Must include:  
  * Heating → expansion → lower density → rising  
  * Cooling → higher density → sinking  
* Use the term **“convection current”**

# **☀️ 2.3.3 Radiation**

## **✅ Definition**

**Radiation** is the transfer of thermal energy by **infrared waves**.

## **🌈 Key Facts**

* All objects emit **infrared radiation**  
* Does **NOT require a medium** → can travel through vacuum

👉 Example: Heat from the Sun reaches Earth through space

## **🎨 Effect of Surface Type**

| Surface | Absorption | Emission | Reflection |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Black, dull | Good | Good | Poor |
| White, shiny | Poor | Poor | Good |

## **🧪 Experiments**

### **🔬 Emitters:**

* Compare black vs shiny surfaces  
* Use thermometer

**Result:**

* Black emits more radiation → cools faster

### **🔬 Absorbers:**

* Shine infrared on surfaces

**Result:**

* Black absorbs more → heats faster

## **🌡️ Energy Balance**

### **Constant Temperature:**

* Energy in \= Energy out

### **If:**

* **Energy in \> Energy out** → temperature rises  
* **Energy in \< Energy out** → temperature falls

## **🌍 Earth’s Temperature (Very Important)**

* Sun emits radiation → Earth absorbs it  
* Earth emits infrared radiation back

### **Affected by:**

* Greenhouse gases (trap heat)  
* Surface properties  
* Clouds

## **📊 Factors Affecting Rate of Radiation**

1. **Temperature**  
   * Higher temperature → more radiation  
2. **Surface area**  
   * Larger area → more emission

## **📌 Exam Tips (Radiation)**

* Always say **infrared radiation**  
* Mention:  
  * No medium required  
  * Travels in vacuum  
* Know surface effects clearly (black vs shiny)

# **⚙️ 2.3.4 Consequences of Thermal Energy Transfer**

## **🍳 (a) Heating Objects (e.g. Pans)**

### **Processes involved:**

* **Conduction:** Heat spreads through metal pan  
* **Convection:** Hot liquid inside moves  
* **Radiation:** Heat emitted from surface

👉 Metal pans are used because they are **good conductors**

## **🏠 (b) Heating a Room**

### **By Convection:**

1. Heater warms air  
2. Warm air rises  
3. Cool air sinks  
4. Circulation heats room

👉 Forms convection currents in the room

## **🔥 Complex Example 1: Fire (Wood/Coal)**

### **All 3 processes:**

* **Radiation:** Heat felt from fire  
* **Conduction:** Heat through solid fuel  
* **Convection:** Hot air rises

## **🚗 Complex Example 2: Car Radiator**

### **How it works:**

* Hot coolant flows into radiator  
* Heat lost by:  
  * **Conduction** (through metal)  
  * **Convection** (air flow)  
  * **Radiation**

👉 Fans increase convection → faster cooling

# **🧠 Summary Table**

| Method | Medium Needed? | Where Occurs | Mechanism |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Conduction | Yes | Solids | Particle vibration \+ free electrons |
| Convection | Yes | Liquids & gases | Density changes |
| Radiation | No | Anywhere | Infrared waves |

# **🎯 Final Exam Tips**

* Always identify the **type of heat transfer**  
* Use **correct scientific terms**:  
  * “Infrared radiation”  
  * “Convection current”  
  * “Density decreases”  
* For 3–4 mark questions:  
  * Explain step-by-step (especially convection)  
* Learn **real-life examples** (very common in exams\!)
