# **🌆 Urban Settlements**

## **🔑 Key Definitions**

* **Urban settlement**: A built-up area with a high population density and mainly non-agricultural activities  
* **Urbanisation**: The increase in the proportion of people living in towns and cities  
* **Rural–urban migration**: Movement of people from countryside to cities  
* **Urban sprawl**: The uncontrolled expansion of urban areas into surrounding rural land

# **🏙️ Land Use in Urban Areas**

Cities are divided into **land use zones**, each with specific characteristics.

## **1\. 💼 Central Business District (CBD)**

### **📍 Characteristics:**

* Located at the **centre of the city**  
* High land value → expensive  
* Tall buildings (skyscrapers)  
* Shops, offices, banks  
* Good transport accessibility  
* High pedestrian and traffic flow

### **❓ Why these characteristics?**

* Central location → accessible to most people  
* Limited space → build upwards (high-rise buildings)  
* High demand → high land prices

## **2\. 🏘️ Residential Areas**

### **📍 Characteristics:**

* Located outside the CBD  
* Housing (flats, apartments, houses)  
* Quality varies (high-income vs low-income areas)

### **🧭 Types:**

* **High-income areas** → spacious houses, good services  
* **Low-income areas** → overcrowded housing, fewer services

## **3\. 🏭 Industrial Areas**

### **📍 Characteristics:**

* Factories and warehouses  
* Often located:  
  * Near transport routes (roads, railways, ports)  
  * On cheaper land (edge of city)

### **❓ Why located there?**

* Need space (large buildings)  
* Avoid pollution affecting residential areas  
* Easy transport of goods

## **4\. 🌿 Rural–Urban Fringe**

### **📍 Characteristics:**

* Edge of the city where urban meets rural  
* Mixed land use:  
  * Farms  
  * Housing estates  
  * Airports, landfills, industrial estates

### **❗ Issues:**

* Land-use conflicts  
* Pressure from urban expansion

# **🔄 Changes in Urban Land Use**

Urban areas change over time due to:

### **1\. 🚶‍♂️ Decentralisation**

* Movement of people and businesses away from CBD

### **2\. 🏚️ Urban Decline**

* Old inner-city areas become run-down

### **3\. 🏗️ Urban Renewal**

* Redevelopment of old areas (new buildings, services)

**4\. 🌍 Urban Sprawl**

* Expansion of city into surrounding countryside

## **❗ Effects of Urban Sprawl**

* Loss of farmland  
* Increased traffic  
* Environmental damage  
* Increased commuting

# **⚠️ Problems of Urban Areas**

## **1\. 🌫️ Pollution**

### **Types:**

* **Air pollution** → vehicle emissions, factories  
* **Water pollution** → waste dumped into rivers  
* **Noise pollution** → traffic, construction  
* **Visual pollution** → litter, billboards, poor housing

## **2\. 🚗 Traffic Congestion**

### **Causes:**

* Too many vehicles  
* Poor public transport  
* Narrow roads

### **Effects:**

* Time wasted  
* Increased pollution

**3\. 🏚️ Housing Problems**

### **Causes:**

* Rapid urbanisation  
* Rural–urban migration

### **Effects:**

* Slums/squatter settlements  
* Overcrowding  
* Poor sanitation

## **4\. ⚖️ Inequality**

* Differences in wealth and access to services  
* Rich vs poor neighbourhoods

## **5\. ⚔️ Land Use Conflicts**

* Competition for land:  
  * Housing vs industry  
  * Roads vs green spaces

# **🛠️ Solutions to Urban Problems**

## **🌱 Pollution Solutions**

* Clean energy  
* Emission controls  
* Recycling

## **🚉 Traffic Solutions**

* Improve public transport  
* Build bypasses/ring roads  
* Encourage cycling/walking

## **🏠 Housing Solutions**

* Build affordable housing  
* Upgrade slums (water, sanitation)  
* Government planning

## **⚖️ Reducing Inequality**

* Improve access to education and healthcare  
* Job creation

## **🌳 Managing Urban Sprawl**

* Green belts (protected land)  
* Urban planning policies

# **🌍 Case Study: Urban Area**

## **🇰🇪 Nairobi (Kenya)**

### **📊 Key Facts**

* Capital city  
* Rapid urban growth  
* Major economic centre in East Africa

## **🏙️ Land Use**

**CBD:**

* Shops, offices, banks  
* High-rise buildings  
* Heavy traffic

**Residential Areas:**

* Wealthy areas (e.g. Karen) → large houses  
* Low-income areas (e.g. Kibera) → informal settlements

**Industrial Areas:**

* Located on outskirts  
* Factories and warehouses

**Rural–Urban Fringe:**

* Expansion into surrounding land  
* Mixed land uses

## **⚠️ Urban Problems**

* Traffic congestion  
* Air pollution  
* Housing shortages (slums)  
* Inequality  
* Waste management issues

## **🛠️ Solutions**

* Road expansion projects  
* Improved public transport  
* Slum upgrading projects  
* Waste management initiatives

# **📝 Exam Tips**

## **💡 For “Describe land use”**

* Name zones (CBD, residential, etc.)  
* Give characteristics  
* Use examples

## **💡 For “Explain changes”**

* Give reasons (e.g. urban sprawl due to population growth)  
* Include effects

## **💡 For “Problems & solutions”**

* Always:  
  * State problem  
  * Explain cause  
  * Give solution

## **💡 For case study questions:**

* Include:  
  * Named place (e.g. Nairobi)  
  * Specific details (not general statements)  
  * Data if possible

## **❗ Common mistakes**

* Only listing problems (no explanation)  
* Forgetting solutions  
* Not linking cause → effect

# **📌 Quick Summary**

* Urban areas have different **land use zones**:  
  * CBD, residential, industrial, rural–urban fringe  
* Urban areas change due to:  
  * Growth, migration, and planning  
* Key problems:  
  * Pollution, congestion, housing, inequality  
* Solutions:  
  * Planning, infrastructure, sustainable development
