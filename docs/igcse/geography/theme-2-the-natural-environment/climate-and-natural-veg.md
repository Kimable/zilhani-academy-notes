# **🌍 CIE IGCSE Geography: Climate & Natural Vegetation**

# **1\. 🌦️ Key Definitions**

* **Climate**: Average weather over a long period (30+ years)  
* **Natural vegetation**: Plants that grow naturally without human interference  
* **Ecosystem**: A community of plants, animals, soil, and climate interacting together

# **2\. 🌡️ Factors Affecting Climate**

## **🔹 1\. Latitude**

* Near Equator → hotter (direct sunlight)  
* Near poles → colder (slanted rays)

## **🔹 2\. Pressure Systems & Winds**

* **Low pressure** → rising air → rain  
* **High pressure** → sinking air → dry

## **🔹 3\. Distance from the Sea**

* Coastal areas → mild, wetter  
* Inland areas → extreme temperatures, drier

## **🔹 4\. Ocean Currents**

* Warm currents → increase temperature & rainfall  
* Cold currents → reduce rainfall

## **🔹 5\. Altitude**

* Higher altitude → cooler temperatures

# **3\. 🌴 Equatorial Climate**

## **🔹 Location**

* Around the Equator (0°–10° N/S)

👉 Example: Amazon Basin

## **🔹 Climate Characteristics**

### **🌡️ Temperature**

* High all year (\~25–28°C)  
* Very small annual range (\~2–3°C)

### **🌧️ Rainfall**

* Very high (\>2000 mm per year)  
* Rain all year (no dry season)

## **🔹 Rainfall Type: Convectional Rainfall**

### **Formation:**

1. Sun heats ground  
2. Warm air rises  
3. Air cools → condensation  
4. Heavy rain (often thunderstorms)

## **🔹 Climate Graph Features**

* Flat temperature line  
* High rainfall bars every month

# **4\. 🌵 Hot Desert Climate**

## **🔹 Location**

* 15°–30° N/S (Tropics)

👉 Example: Sahara Desert

## **🔹 Climate Characteristics**

### **🌡️ Temperature**

* Very hot days (30–50°C)  
* Cool nights  
* Large daily and annual range

### **🌧️ Rainfall**

* Very low (\<250 mm per year)  
* Irregular and unpredictable

## **🔹 Reasons for Dry Conditions**

* **High pressure** → sinking air (no clouds)  
* **Distance from sea**  
* **Cold ocean currents**  
* **Rain shadow effect**

## **🔹 Rainfall Type: Relief Rainfall (limited)**

Occurs when:

* Air forced over mountains → cools → rain (rare in deserts)

## **🔹 Climate Graph Features**

* Very low rainfall bars  
* Large temperature variation

# **5\. 🌳 Tropical Rainforest Ecosystem**

## **🔹 Location**

👉 Amazon Rainforest

## **🔹 Vegetation Characteristics**

* Dense, evergreen forest  
* Layered structure:  
  * Emergent layer  
  * Canopy  
  * Understory  
  * Forest floor

### **Adaptations:**

* Tall trees → compete for light  
* Drip-tip leaves → shed water  
* Buttress roots → support tall trees

## **🔹 Soil**

* Thin and nutrient-poor  
* Nutrients stored in vegetation  
* Rapid decomposition

## **🔹 Wildlife**

* Very high biodiversity  
* Examples:  
  * Monkeys  
  * Birds  
  * Insects

## **🔹 Climate Relationship**

* High rainfall \+ heat → rapid plant growth

# **6\. 🏜️ Hot Desert Ecosystem**

## **🔹 Location**

👉 Sahara Desert

## **🔹 Vegetation Characteristics**

* Sparse vegetation  
* Widely spaced plants

### **Adaptations:**

* Small/spiky leaves → reduce water loss  
* Deep roots → reach groundwater  
* Thick stems → store water

## **🔹 Soil**

* Sandy or rocky  
* Very low organic content

## **🔹 Wildlife**

* Limited but adapted:  
  * Camels  
  * Lizards  
  * Scorpions

## **🔹 Climate Relationship**

* Low rainfall → limited plant growth

# **7\. 🌳 Deforestation (Tropical Rainforest)**

## **🔹 Causes**

1. Logging (timber)  
2. Farming (subsistence & commercial)  
3. Cattle ranching  
4. Mining  
5. Road building

## **🔹 Effects on Environment**

### **Local Effects:**

* Loss of biodiversity  
* Soil erosion  
* Flooding

### **Global Effects:**

* Increased CO₂ → climate change  
* Reduced carbon sink

## **🔹 Effects on People**

* Loss of indigenous homes  
* Jobs created (logging, farming)  
* Long-term loss of resources

# **8\. 📊 Climate Graph Interpretation**

## **🔹 Equatorial Graph**

* Constant high temperature  
* High rainfall all year

## **🔹 Desert Graph**

* Low rainfall  
* Large temperature range

## **🔹 What to Look For:**

* Wettest/driest months  
* Temperature range  
* Seasonal patterns

# **9\. ⚠️ Impacts on People & Environment**

## **🔹 Tropical Rainforest**

### **Opportunities:**

* Tourism  
* Timber  
* Medicines

### **Challenges:**

* Difficult farming  
* Disease  
* Transport issues

## **🔹 Hot Desert**

### **Opportunities:**

* Oil extraction  
* Tourism  
* Solar energy

### **Challenges:**

* Water shortage  
* Extreme temperatures  
* Sparse population
