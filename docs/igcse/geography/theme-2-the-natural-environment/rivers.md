# **🌊 Rivers**

## **🔑 Key Definitions**

* **River**: A natural flow of water in a channel from source to mouth  
* **Drainage basin**: The area of land drained by a river and its tributaries  
* **Watershed**: The boundary separating one drainage basin from another  
* **Tributary**: A smaller river that joins a larger one  
* **Confluence**: The point where two rivers meet

# **🌧️ Hydrological Processes in a Drainage Basin**

These processes explain how water moves through a drainage basin.

## **💧 Key Processes**

### **1\. 🌿 Interception**

* Rainfall caught by vegetation before reaching the ground

### **2\. 🌱 Infiltration**

* Water soaking into the soil

### **3\. 🌊 Throughflow**

* Movement of water through soil towards the river

### **4\. 💧 Groundwater Flow**

* Slow movement of water through rock underground

### **5\. ☀️ Evaporation**

* Water turning into vapour due to heat

### **6\. 🏃‍♂️ Overland Flow (Surface Runoff)**

* Water flowing over the surface into rivers

👉 Occurs when:

* Soil is saturated  
* Ground is impermeable

# **🌊 River Characteristics**

## **📏 Key Features**

* **Width** → increases downstream  
* **Depth** → increases downstream  
* **Velocity (speed)** → increases downstream  
* **Discharge** → volume of water flowing per second

👉 Example:

* River Nile becomes wider and deeper towards its mouth

---

# **🔄 The Work of a River**

---

## **1\. ⛏️ Erosion**

### **Types:**

* **Hydraulic action** → force of water breaks rock  
* **Abrasion (corrasion)** → rocks scrape river bed  
* **Attrition** → rocks collide and break into smaller pieces  
* **Solution (corrosion)** → soluble rocks dissolve

---

## **2\. 🚚 Transportation**

### **Types:**

* **Traction** → large rocks rolled along bed  
* **Saltation** → small stones bounce  
* **Suspension** → fine particles carried  
* **Solution** → dissolved material carried

---

## **3\. 🏝️ Deposition**

* Occurs when river loses energy  
* Sediment is dropped

👉 Happens when:

* Velocity decreases  
* River enters sea/lake  
* Flooding occurs

---

# **🏞️ River Landforms**

---

## **🟢 Upper Course (near source)**

### **📍 Features:**

* Steep gradient  
* Narrow, shallow channel

---

### **🌊 Waterfalls**

* Form where hard rock overlays soft rock  
* Soft rock erodes faster → step forms

👉 Example:

* Victoria Falls

---

### **🌀 Potholes**

* Form by swirling stones eroding river bed

---

## **🟡 Middle Course**

### **📍 Features:**

* Gentler gradient  
* Wider, deeper channel

---

### **🌊 Meanders**

* Bends in the river

👉 Formation:

* Outer bend → erosion  
* Inner bend → deposition

---

## **🔵 Lower Course (near mouth)**

### **📍 Features:**

* Very wide, deep channel  
* Very gentle slope

---

### **🌙 Oxbow Lakes**

* Form when a meander is cut off

---

### **🌾 Floodplains**

* Flat land beside river  
* Formed by deposition during floods

---

### **🏝️ Levees**

* Raised river banks formed by deposition

---

### **🌊 Deltas**

* Form where river meets sea  
* Deposition builds land outward

👉 Example:

* Nile Delta

---

# **⚠️ River Hazards**

---

## **🌊 Flooding**

### **Causes:**

**Natural:**

* Heavy rainfall  
* Snowmelt  
* Impermeable rock

**Human:**

* Deforestation  
* Urbanisation (more runoff)  
* Building on floodplains

---

## **⛏️ River Erosion**

* Undercutting banks  
* Collapse of land

---

# **🌱 Opportunities of Rivers**

---

## **✅ Benefits**

* Water supply (drinking, irrigation)  
* Fertile soils (floodplains)  
* Transport routes  
* Hydroelectric power (HEP)  
* Tourism

👉 Example:

* River Thames supports transport and tourism

---

# **🛠️ Managing River Flooding**

---

## **🧱 Hard Engineering**

* **Dams** → control flow  
* **Levees** → increase channel capacity  
* **Channel straightening** → speed up flow

---

## **🌱 Soft Engineering**

* Flood warnings  
* Afforestation (planting trees)  
* Zoning (restrict building in flood-prone areas)

---

# **📍 Case Study: River**

## **🌍 River Nile**

---

### **✅ Opportunities**

* Irrigation for farming  
* Transport  
* Hydroelectric power (Aswan Dam)  
* Tourism

---

### **⚠️ Hazards**

* Flooding (historically before dam)  
* Water disputes between countries

---

### **🛠️ Management**

* Aswan High Dam:  
  * Controls flooding  
  * Provides electricity  
  * Stores water

  * Causes  
  * Effects

  * Hazards  
  * Management

## **❗ Common mistakes**

* Mixing up erosion, transport, deposition  
* Not explaining formation (just naming landforms)  
* Forgetting examples

---

# **📌 Quick Summary**

* Rivers shape land through:  
  * Erosion  
  * Transportation  
  * Deposition  
* Landforms change from:  
  * Upper → Middle → Lower course  
* Rivers provide:  
  * Opportunities (water, farming, energy)  
  * Hazards (flooding, erosion)  
* Managed by:  
  * Hard and soft engineering
