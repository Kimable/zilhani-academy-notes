# **📘 MARKETING STRATEGY (IGCSE Business Studies)**

## **🔑 1\. What is a Marketing Strategy?**

A **marketing strategy** is a long-term plan to achieve marketing objectives by choosing the right combination of marketing activities.

* It is based on:  
  * Target market  
  * Market research  
  * Business objectives  
  * Budget/resources

👉 It answers:

* Who are we selling to?  
* How will we reach them?  
* Why will they choose our product?

# **🎯 2\. The Marketing Mix (4Ps)**

The **marketing mix** is the combination of:

| Element | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Product | What is being sold |
| Price | How much it costs |
| Place | Where it is sold |
| Promotion | How it is advertised |

## **🧠 Importance of Each Element in Influencing Consumer Decisions**

### **1\. Product**

* Quality, design, branding, packaging  
* Determines customer satisfaction

**Example:**

* A smartphone with better camera → attracts young consumers

**Key idea:**  
👉 If product doesn’t meet needs → no sales regardless of price/promotion

### **2\. Price**

* Must match target market affordability  
* Influences demand

**Pricing strategies:**

* Penetration pricing (low price to enter market)  
* Price skimming (high price initially)  
* Competitive pricing

**Example:**

* Budget brands use low prices to attract price-sensitive customers

### **3\. Place**

* Distribution channels (shops, online, wholesalers)

**Example:**

* Selling via supermarkets vs online stores

**Key idea:**  
👉 Convenience \= higher sales

### **4\. Promotion**

* Advertising, sales promotion, PR, direct marketing

**Example:**

* Discounts, social media ads

**Key idea:**  
👉 Raises awareness and persuades customers

## **🧠 Exam Tip (VERY IMPORTANT)**

When asked:

“Which element is most important?”

👉 DO NOT just describe — **you must justify**:

Example:

* “Price is most important because the target market is low-income, so affordability is key…”

# **⚖️ 3\. Justifying Marketing Strategies in Different Situations**

You must:

1. Identify the situation  
2. Choose appropriate strategy  
3. Justify with reasons

## **🔍 Common Situations & Strategies**

### **🆕 New Product Launch**

* Heavy promotion  
* Penetration pricing  
* Attractive packaging

👉 Why?

* Need awareness \+ trial purchases

### **💰 Targeting High-Income Customers**

* Premium pricing  
* High quality product  
* Exclusive distribution

👉 Why?

* Customers associate high price with quality

### **📉 In a Competitive Market**

* Competitive pricing  
* Strong branding  
* Promotion offers

👉 Why?

* Need to stand out from rivals

### **🌍 Expanding Sales**

* New distribution channels  
* Online selling

👉 Why?

* Reach more customers

## **🧠 Evaluation Tip**

Always include:

* “This depends on…”  
* “However…”  
* “In the long term…”

# **⚖️ 4\. Legal Controls on Marketing**

Governments impose laws to protect consumers.

## **📜 Types of Legal Controls**

### **1\. Misleading Promotion**

* False advertising is illegal

**Example:**

* Claiming a product cures diseases when it doesn’t

👉 Impact:

* Businesses must ensure accuracy  
* Limits exaggeration in ads

### **2\. Faulty and Dangerous Goods**

* Products must be safe and fit for purpose

**Example:**

* Electrical goods must not cause harm

👉 Impact:

* Higher production costs (quality control)  
* Possible recalls if faulty

## **📉 Impact on Marketing Strategy**

| Positive | Negative |
| ----- | ----- |
| Builds trust | Increases costs |
| Protects brand image | Limits promotional claims |
| Avoids legal penalties | Slows product launch |

## **🧠 Exam Tip**

Questions often ask:

“How do legal controls affect marketing?”

👉 Structure:

1. Identify law  
2. Explain impact  
3. Link to marketing decision

# **🌍 5\. Entering New Foreign Markets**

## **📈 Growth Potential**

Benefits:

* More customers  
* Increased sales revenue  
* Reduced dependence on one country

**Example:**

* Expanding into emerging markets with growing populations

## **⚠️ Problems of Entering Foreign Markets**

### **1\. Cultural Differences**

* Different tastes, language, religion

**Example:**

* Food products may not match local preferences

### **2\. Lack of Knowledge**

* Unfamiliar market conditions  
* Different laws

### **3\. Costs**

* Transport  
* Setting up operations

### **4\. Competition**

* Strong local businesses

## **🔧 Methods to Overcome Problems**

### **🤝 1\. Joint Ventures**

* Partner with a local business

**Benefits:**

* Local knowledge  
* Shared risk

**Limitations:**

* Shared profits  
* Possible conflict

### **📜 2\. Licensing**

* Allow another firm to produce your product

**Benefits:**

* Low cost  
* Fast entry

**Limitations:**

* Less control  
* Risk of poor quality

## **🧠 Exam Tip**

When asked:

“Recommend a method…”

👉 Structure:

1. Choose method  
2. Give advantages  
3. Give limitations  
4. Final judgement

# **📝 6\. How to Answer 6–8 Mark Questions (IMPORTANT)**

Use this structure:

### **✅ Step-by-step:**

1. **Identify the situation**  
2. **Apply knowledge**  
3. **Explain impact**  
4. **Compare options**  
5. **Make a justified conclusion**

### **✍️ Example Question:**

Recommend a marketing strategy for a new product.

**Answer structure:**

* Use promotion \+ penetration pricing  
* Explain why (attract customers)  
* Consider alternative (skimming)  
* Conclude with best option

# **🚀 7\. Key Terms (Must Memorize)**

* **Marketing strategy** – plan to achieve marketing objectives  
* **Marketing mix** – product, price, place, promotion  
* **Target market** – group of customers aimed at  
* **Penetration pricing** – low price to enter market  
* **Price skimming** – high initial price  
* **Joint venture** – partnership with another firm  
* **Licensing** – allowing another firm to produce goods

# **🧠 Final Exam Tips**

✔ Always **use the case study context**  
✔ Avoid generic answers  
✔ Include **evaluation (pros \+ cons)**  
✔ Use phrases like:

* “This is effective because…”  
* “However…”  
* “Therefore the best option is…”
