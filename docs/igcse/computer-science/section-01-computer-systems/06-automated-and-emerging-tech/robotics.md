# **🤖 ROBOTICS – CIE IGCSE COMPUTER SCIENCE NOTES**

## **1️⃣ What is Meant by Robotics?**

### **🔹 Definition**

**Robotics** is a branch of computer science that involves the **design, construction, and operation of robots**.

It combines:

* Computer Science  
* Engineering  
* Electronics  
* Artificial Intelligence

In simple terms:

Robotics is the field concerned with creating and programming machines (robots) that can perform tasks automatically.

## **2️⃣ What is a Robot?**

### **🔹 Definition**

A **robot** is a programmable machine that can carry out tasks automatically by interacting with its environment using sensors and actuators.

A robot:

* Can sense its surroundings  
* Can process information  
* Can act based on instructions or programming  
* Often works automatically without constant human control

## **3️⃣ Characteristics of a Robot**

For the exam, you should be able to describe these key features:

### **✅ 1\. Mechanical Structure (Framework)**

* The physical body of the robot.  
* Includes arms, wheels, joints, casing, etc.  
* Allows movement or physical interaction.

Example:

* A robotic arm in a factory.  
* The body and propellers of a drone.

### **✅ 2\. Electrical Components**

Robots contain electronic parts that allow them to function:

#### **a) Sensors**

* Detect changes in the environment.  
* Input devices.

Examples:

* Temperature sensor  
* Light sensor  
* Motion sensor  
* Proximity sensor  
* Camera

#### **b) Microprocessor (Control Unit)**

* The "brain" of the robot.  
* Processes data from sensors.  
* Makes decisions based on programming.  
* Sends signals to actuators.

#### **c) Actuators**

* Output devices.  
* Convert electrical signals into physical movement.

Examples:

* Motors  
* Hydraulic systems  
* Pneumatic systems

### **✅ 3\. Programmable**

* Robots must be programmable.  
* Instructions are stored in memory.  
* They follow algorithms.

Without programming → it is just a machine, not a robot.

## **4️⃣ Examples of Robots**

The syllabus mentions:

### **🏭 Factory Equipment**

Example:

* Robotic arms assembling cars.

Used by companies like car manufacturers to:

* Weld  
* Paint  
* Assemble parts

### **🏠 Domestic Robots**

Example:

* Robot vacuum cleaners  
* Lawn-mowing robots

They:

* Detect obstacles  
* Navigate rooms  
* Clean automatically

### **🚁 Drones**

Used for:

* Deliveries  
* Photography  
* Surveillance  
* Agriculture monitoring

## **5️⃣ Roles of Robots**

Robots can be used in many sectors:

### **🏭 1\. Industry**

Used for:

* Assembly lines  
* Welding  
* Painting  
* Packaging

Advantages:

* Work 24/7  
* High precision  
* No fatigue

**🚗 2\. Transport**

Examples:

* Self-driving vehicles  
* Delivery drones

Benefits:

* Reduced human error  
* Faster delivery

### **🌾 3\. Agriculture**

Used for:

* Crop monitoring  
* Automatic harvesting  
* Spraying pesticides

Benefits:

* Increased efficiency  
* Reduced labour cost

### **🏥 4\. Medicine**

Examples:

* Surgical robots  
* Rehabilitation robots

Robots assist surgeons with:

* Very precise movements  
* Minimally invasive surgery

### **🏠 5\. Domestic Settings**

Examples:

* Cleaning robots  
* Smart assistants  
* Security robots

### **🎮 6\. Entertainment**

Examples:

* Robotic toys  
* Animatronics in theme parks  
* Robot performers

## **6️⃣ Advantages of Robots**

In exams, write clearly in full sentences.

### **✅ 1\. Work Continuously**

* Can operate 24 hours a day.  
* No need for breaks.

### **✅ 2\. High Accuracy**

* Very precise.  
* Reduces human error.

### **✅ 3\. Perform Dangerous Tasks**

* Work in extreme heat.  
* Handle toxic materials.  
* Work in space or underwater.

### **✅ 4\. Increased Productivity**

* Faster than humans.  
* Improve efficiency.

### **✅ 5\. Consistency**

* Perform tasks exactly the same way each time.

## **7️⃣ Disadvantages of Robots**

Important for evaluation questions.

### **❌ 1\. Expensive**

* High initial cost.  
* Maintenance costs.

### **❌ 2\. Job Loss**

* Can replace human workers.  
* Leads to unemployment in some sectors.

### **❌ 3\. Limited to Programming**

* Cannot think independently (unless AI-based).  
* Only perform what they are programmed to do.

### **❌ 4\. Technical Failures**

* System errors.  
* Power failure.  
* Software bugs.

### **❌ 5\. Security Risks**

* Can be hacked.  
* Risk in military or transport robots.

# **✏️ Exam Tips (Very Important for CIE)**

### **🔹 Tip 1: Use Correct Terminology**

Always use words like:

* Sensor  
* Actuator  
* Microprocessor  
* Programmable

Markers reward technical vocabulary.

### **🔹 Tip 2: Explain Clearly**

Instead of writing:

Robots are useful in factories.

Write:

Robots are used in factories to assemble products with high accuracy and speed.

### **🔹 Tip 3: Advantages vs Disadvantages Questions**

If question says:

Discuss the use of robots in industry (6 marks)

Structure:

* 3 advantages  
* 3 disadvantages  
* Full sentences  
* Clear explanation

### **🔹 Tip 4: Know the Structure of a Robot**

If asked:

Describe the characteristics of a robot

You must include:

* Mechanical structure  
* Sensors  
* Microprocessor  
* Actuators  
* Programmable

Missing one may lose marks.

# **🧠 Comparison: Robot vs Normal Machine**

| Robot | Normal Machine |
| ----- | ----- |
| Programmable | Not necessarily programmable |
| Uses sensors | May not use sensors |
| Can make decisions | Just follows fixed mechanism |
