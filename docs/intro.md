# Introduction

Welcome to Zilhani Notes, a comprehensive collection of study materials and resources for students preparing for their IGCSE exams.

This website is designed to provide students with high-quality notes, summaries, and practice questions across a wide range of subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, and Business Studies.

Our goal is to help students succeed in their exams by providing clear and concise explanations of key concepts,as well as practical examples and exercises to reinforce learning.

Whether you're looking for a quick review or in-depth study materials, Zilhani Notes has you covered. Explore our subjects and start your journey towards academic success today!
